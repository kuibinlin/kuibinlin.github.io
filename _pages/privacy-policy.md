**Last updated:** 27 April 2026

Welcome to linsnotes.com. This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, and protect your information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

## 1. Information I Collect

### a. Personal Information

I collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

- Email address (when you subscribe to updates)

### b. Usage Data

I may collect non-personal data automatically when you visit the site, such as:

- IP address (which may be anonymised)
- Browser type and device information
- Pages visited and time spent

## 2. How I Use Your Information

I use your information to:

- Send newsletters and updates
- Improve the website and its content
- Monitor and analyse usage

## 3. Email Subscriptions

I use Brevo to manage email subscriptions. By signing up, your email address will be stored and processed by Brevo.

You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.

## 4. Analytics and Tracking

I use the following services:

- Google Analytics
- GoatCounter

These services help me understand how visitors use the site. They may collect usage data and use cookies.

## 5. Infrastructure and Security

I use Cloudflare to improve performance and security. This may involve processing IP addresses and traffic data.

## 6. Cookies

I may use cookies to improve your browsing experience and analyse traffic. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

## 7. Data Retention

I retain personal data only as long as necessary:

- Email data: until you unsubscribe
- Analytics data: according to provider policies

## 8. Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your own, depending on the service providers used. I take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw your consent.

## 10. Data Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## 12. Contact

If you have any questions or requests, please contact me at [hello@linsnotes.com](mailto:hello@linsnotes.com)
